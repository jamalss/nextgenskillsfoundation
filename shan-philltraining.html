Accreditation

Licensed and government-approved.

SHAN-PHILL Training operates under an official Private Educational Institution Permit from Saint Lucia’s Ministry of Education, and is an approved centre of the Saint Lucia Council for Technical and Vocational Education and Training (SLCTVET).

This TVET training-provider recognition is held by Shanel Phillip as an individual, sole-proprietor training provider, a status typically held by larger organizations such as NELU and NSDC. She is currently the only individual on record in Saint Lucia recognized this way.

Ministry of Education Permit

Private Educational Institution Permit granted May 10, 2025, valid through May 9, 2028. Licensed course areas: Electrical Installation, Community Tourism, Customer Services, and Supervisory Management.

SLCTVET Approved Centre

Centre Number SPHT23-04/25. Approval period May 2025 – April 2028, issued by the Saint Lucia Council for Technical and Vocational Education and Training.

Featured programme

Electrical Installation, Level 1, 2 & 3

A hands-on vocational pathway for anyone looking to build a practical trade skill from the ground up, with Level 2 and Level 3 tracks for those ready to go further.

  • Who it’s for: anyone starting or building a trade skill in electrical work
  • What to expect: practical installation training over a 3–4 month programme per level
  • Progression: Level 1 leads into a Level 2 programme, and Level 2 to Level 3
  • Already have experience? You’ll be assessed first to confirm your level, so you can start at Level 2 or 3 if you qualify
