Accreditation & certification

Officially approved and permitted to train.

Beyond endorsement letters, Shan-Phill Training operates under a Ministry of Education permit and is certified as an approved centre by SLCTVET — the two credentials that put its CVQ programmes on an official footing.

SLCTVET Approved Centre

Shan-Phill Training is certified as an Approved Centre by the Saint Lucia Council for Technical and Vocational Education and Training (SLCTVET), the national body responsible for CVQ certification standards.

  • Centre number: SPHT23-04/25
  • Approval period: May 2025 – April 2028
  • Issued by: The Saint Lucia Council for Technical and Vocational Education and Training
Download original image ↓

Private Educational Institution Permit

Shan-Phill Training holds a Private Educational Institution Permit from the Ministry of Education, granted under the Saint Lucia Education Act, authorizing it to deliver training leading to CVQ certification.

  • Issued by: Ministry of Education, Sustainable Development, Innovation, Science, Technology and Vocational Training
  • Approved courses: Electrical Installation (all levels), Community Tourism, Customer Service, Supervisory Management
  • Permit period: May 10, 2025 – May 9, 2028
