This page explains, in plain terms, how NextGen Skills Foundation and Shan-Phill Training each collect and use information through this website, and sets out the terms for using the site. The two organizations share a founder and a web domain, but are separate legal entities, each responsible only for the data collected through its own page — see Section 1 for which organization controls what. This page is written to satisfy GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and Caribbean data-protection expectations for an internationally-facing site, but it is a compliance template adapted to these organizations' actual practices — not a substitute for advice from a qualified attorney. If either organization's data practices change materially (for example, a new third-party tool is added), this page should be reviewed and updated before that change goes live.

1. Who We Are

This Legal & Privacy page covers two separate organizations that share this web domain and this one policy page. Each is its own "data controller" for the data collected through its own page — one organization is not responsible for the other's data practices.

NextGen Skills Foundation

NextGen Skills Foundation is a nonprofit organization based in Saint Lucia, recognized by Saint Lucia's Ministry of Equity, Social Justice, Gender, Older Persons, Labour, Cooperatives and Consumer Affairs as operating in compliance with the Non-Governmental Organizations Act. It designs and facilitates vocational skills-development projects for young people. Its page is nextgenskillsfoundation.com, and it is the data controller for personal data submitted through that page (Sections 2–3, 5–6 describe what it collects). You can reach it at nextgenskillsfoundation@gmail.com.

Shan-Phill Training

Shan-Phill Training is a private skills-training and certification provider based in Saint Lucia, offering CVQ, certificate and professional-development courses. It shares a founder with NextGen Skills Foundation, but is a separate business and a separate data controller. Its page is nextgenskillsfoundation.com/shan-philltraining.html, and it is the data controller for information handled through that page (see Sections 2–3 and 6 for how its enquiry/application process differs from NextGen's). You can reach it at shanphilltraining@gmail.com or 1-758-712-7876.

2. What Data We Collect

Google Analytics 4 and the CookieYes consent banner run identically across every page on this domain — the NextGen Skills Foundation homepage, the Recognition & Endorsements page, and the Shan-Phill Training page. When active, GA4 collects standard analytics data such as pages viewed, approximate location (city/country level, derived from IP address), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. This data is pseudonymous — GA4 is not used to identify you personally — and is only collected after you accept analytics cookies through the CookieYes banner described in Section 5. If you decline, none of it is collected on any page.

NextGen Skills Foundation

When you use the "Get Involved" form on the NextGen Skills Foundation homepage, it asks for: the option that best describes you (prospective participant, parent or guardian, school or facility, partner or sponsor, or volunteer), your full name, your email address, an optional organization name, and any message you choose to write. That form submission is sent to NextGen Skills Foundation via Formspree (Section 6) and is not visible to Shan-Phill Training. It does not ask for payment details, government ID numbers, or any information beyond what that form requests.

Shan-Phill Training

The Shan-Phill Training page does not use a server-side form. Its "Apply / Register" tool is a client-side wizard: it asks which programme you're interested in and your name and contact details, and uses that information only to pre-fill a WhatsApp message. Nothing is transmitted to Shan-Phill Training, NextGen Skills Foundation, or any server when you fill in the wizard — the information stays in your own browser until you press "Send" and are handed off to WhatsApp, at which point WhatsApp (Meta) receives and delivers the message under its own privacy policy, linked in Section 6. If you close the page or never press "Send," nothing is collected by anyone. This is unaffected by the site-wide GA4/CookieYes setup described above, which governs analytics only, not this wizard.

3. Why We Collect It

Where you consent to analytics cookies, GA4 data collected across every page on this domain is used to understand, in aggregate, how visitors use the site so it can be improved — for example, which sections are most useful and where visitors are located.

NextGen Skills Foundation collects information to:

  • Respond to inquiries, referrals and applications submitted through the Get Involved form.
  • Keep track of programme participants, referring schools, partners and sponsors.

Shan-Phill Training uses the information entered into its Apply/Register wizard only to build the WhatsApp message you choose to send — to identify which programme you're asking about and how to reach you. It is not stored, logged or analyzed anywhere by Shan-Phill Training beyond the resulting WhatsApp conversation.

Neither organization sells personal data or uses the information you submit for advertising or profiling.

4. Legal Basis for Processing GDPR — Art. 6

For visitors in the EU, EEA or UK, our legal bases for processing personal data under the GDPR are:

  • Consent — for analytics cookies (GA4), which only load after you accept them through the CookieYes consent banner.
  • Legitimate interest — for responding to messages you send us through the Get Involved form, where our interest in running the Foundation's programmes is balanced against your privacy rights.
  • Consent — where you voluntarily submit a message, application or referral through the form, you are consenting to us using that information to respond.

You may withdraw consent at any time — for cookies, by reopening the consent settings described in Section 5; for form submissions, by emailing NextGen Skills Foundation at the address in Section 19.

For Shan-Phill Training, no data is processed until you press "Send" in the Apply/Register wizard and hand the resulting message to WhatsApp yourself — at that point, the legal basis is your own consent to make contact, given by that action.

11. Caribbean Residents' Rights

NextGen Skills Foundation is based in Saint Lucia and most of our participants, partners and visitors are in the Caribbean. Several Caribbean jurisdictions have their own data protection frameworks — for example Jamaica's Data Protection Act, Trinidad and Tobago's Data Protection Act, and Bermuda's Personal Information Protection Act. Where you have rights under the law of your own jurisdiction to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data, we honor those requests on the same terms described for GDPR in Section 8, contacted via the details in Section 19.

12. Children's Privacy

Neither page on this site is directed at children under 13, and neither organization knowingly collects personal information directly from a child under 13. NextGen Skills Foundation's programmes serve young people generally aged 15 and older; where a prospective participant is a minor, NextGen expects a parent, guardian or referring school to be involved in the inquiry, application and enrollment process. Shan-Phill Training's programmes are trade and certificate training generally aimed at working-age adults. If either organization learns that a child under 13 has submitted personal information directly, it will delete it. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information to either organization can contact the relevant one using the details in Section 19 to request its removal.

13. Commercial Email

NextGen Skills Foundation does not currently operate an email marketing list or send promotional email campaigns — email is used only to respond to inquiries submitted through the Get Involved form. If that changes in the future, any commercial email we send will comply with the US CAN-SPAM Act: accurate sender information, a clear subject line, our physical location, and a working way to opt out of future messages.

14. Accessibility

NextGen Skills Foundation is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people using assistive technology. This site is built to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, including: descriptive alternative text on meaningful images, sufficient color contrast between text and background, full keyboard navigability, semantic landmark structure for screen readers, and visible focus indicators on every interactive element. If you encounter an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, please tell us using the details in Section 19 so we can fix it.

16. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption enforced on every page (TLS/SSL).
  • A strict Content Security Policy restricting which scripts and resources this site can load.
  • No storage of payment information — this site does not process payments.
  • No local database of visitor personal information; form submissions are handled by Formspree and delivered to us by email.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. In the unlikely event of a data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify affected individuals and, where required, relevant supervisory authorities without undue delay (within 72 hours under GDPR where feasible).
