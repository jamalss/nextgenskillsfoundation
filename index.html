About the foundation

Skills open doors that circumstance can close.

NextGen Skills Foundation is a Saint Lucia nonprofit that designs and facilitates skills-development projects for young people, with a focus on practical training, recognized certification, and the personal development that helps someone actually use it.

  • Equip students with practical vocational skills
  • Enhance employability through CVQ certification
  • Promote career awareness and personal development
  • Foster industry and community partnerships
  • Support social inclusion and self-reliance

Featured programme

The Circuit & Chill Rehabilitation Skills Project

A seven-month CVQ Level 1 training programme in Electrical Installation and Refrigeration & Air Conditioning, delivered in partnership with the Sandals Foundation and Shan-Phill Training.

In progress · through mid-2026

Equipping incarcerated youth with industry-recognized skills and a pathway to reintegration.

Implemented in partnership with NextGen Skills Foundation and delivered by accredited training provider Shan-Phill Training, the programme supports 30 young men, aged 15 to 35, from the Bordelais Correctional Facility and the Boys Training Centre. Training blends classroom instruction, hands-on workshops and simulated on-the-job experience — aligned with CARICOM-approved standards and certified through the Saint Lucia Council for Technical and Vocational Education and Training (SLCTVET).

EC$135,000
Investment committed by the Sandals Foundation
30
Young men, aged 15–35, currently enrolled
7 months

Beyond technical training, the programme includes a strong psychosocial component — weekly sessions in life skills, inner healing, motivational coaching and financial literacy — helping participants reframe their goals as they build a trade.

“We're not just building skills — we're developing the whole person. The young men are beginning to approach life in a more professional way, and the life-skills component is not only making them better workers, but better individuals.”

Shanel Phillip — President, NextGen Skills Foundation
