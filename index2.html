What We Do

Five ways we invest in Saint Lucia's next generation

Everything we fund and deliver ladders up to these five commitments.

Practical vocational skills

Hands-on training in real trades — electrical installation, refrigeration & air conditioning, and more — that a young person can build a livelihood on.

CVQ certification

Training leads to a real, portable Caribbean Vocational Qualification — not just a certificate of attendance.

Career awareness & personal development

Beyond technical skill — coaching in the workplace habits and mindset that keep a young person employed.

Industry & community partnerships

Programs built and funded together with the businesses and organizations that go on to hire our graduates.

Social inclusion & self-reliance

Priority to vulnerable youth and communities who are usually last to get this kind of opportunity — not first.

Electrical Installation

Summer Skills Workshop for Vulnerable Form 4 Students

A two-week introduction to Electrical Installation for Form 4 students, held at Castries Comprehensive Secondary School — giving students a first real taste of a trade before they leave secondary school.

  • 7th – 18th July, 9am – 2pm
  • Castries Comprehensive Secondary School
